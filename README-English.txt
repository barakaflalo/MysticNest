MysticNest 2.6

Open index.html to preview the app. For installation and offline PWA use, publish all files together at the existing HTTPS address. Open once online, accept the update from the Tarot screen if offered, and finish saving the deck. The shell and translation files are cached together. AI is optional and requires a connection.

Settings > Language offers Hebrew, English, Russian, Spanish and Arabic. Hebrew and English include local readings. The other three languages have translated controls and English content. Write dreams in English for English local matching; select Hebrew for Hebrew matching. The local engine uses known phrases and rules, so it can miss or misunderstand details. Follow-up questions and identification corrections help refine the reading.

Included: 78 illustrated cards and card back; upright and reversed short and expanded meanings; 210 dream symbols/situations including 72 scenarios; 42 combinations; 12 ordered sequences; 8 follow-up groups; journal, tags, favorites, explicit personal associations and feedback; 30 daily fortune messages.

Changing language preserves the current dream text, question, context fields and drawn cards in this tab. Reopen the dream reading with the interpret button or journal. Previously saved entries, tags and personal notes stay in their original words and language. Export a backup in Settings to keep a separate copy of your records. Browser storage is not a permanent backup.

The standalone deck.html gallery has a Hebrew/English toggle. The privacy policy is also available in Hebrew and English.

No remote translation service, additional image downloads or AI call is required for the translations. All illustrations retain their existing IDs and files. Original Downloads files were not changed.
